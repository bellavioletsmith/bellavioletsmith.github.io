BELLA.SMITH DIGITAL DOCUMENTARY COMMERCIAL ETC.

an archival birthday video 





04.2023
CONCEPT: comb through 40 hours of newly digitized home videos to pull together a video that makes my sister weep and cements my status as a Good Sister in the Smith Family.


SET UP: 4 days of organizing footage and compiling selects. 5 days of editing. a few hours of tweaking color for YT.
New York, NY
NOTES: organization was key here. Most of my time was spent organizing footage and preparing subclips.

PERSONAL: I was absolutely moved recovering these videos. my dad has such a powerful documentarian instinct, it’s shocking. He captures such delicate, precious moments and narrates much of it.
