The Sunrise Hunger Strike





11.2021
Washington, DC
CONCEPT: ditch school and join the Sunrise Movement’s media team to document their biggest action: a hunger strike protesting Sen. Manchin and Sen. Sinema’s blocking of Build Back Better’s climate initiatives.
SET UP: lived with 30 other organizers in an airbnb, pumping out daily media content while documenting hunger strikers.  
NOTES: I have 70+ hours of intimate footage and interviews + archival content and newsclips. I began the longer project of creating a mid-sized documentary, though paused it to tend to other projects. I can’t rest until I complete it.
