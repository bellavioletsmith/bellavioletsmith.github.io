  I DON’T LOVE YOU ANYMORE 






04.2022 & 06.2023
CONCEPT: that moment when you realize you gotta leave your man :/



INSPIRATION:the pastel palates and equally mannered subjects and cinematography of DP Luis Perez.
Durham, NC
NOTES: my first scripted fiction piece. it was a super worthwhile exercise in sticking to a highly stylized aesthetic vision.

SET UP: 3 production days. 6 cast members. 4 crew members. 14K of production equipment. Shot on s-log 3 on Sony a7iii.Edited on Premiere. Graded in Davinci.
